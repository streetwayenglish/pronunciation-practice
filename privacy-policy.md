# Privacy Policy

**Emma Speak** helps people practice English pronunciation and conversation. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

**Effective date:** September 9, 2026

## 1. Information We Use

### Voice recordings

When you start a pronunciation or conversation exercise, Emma Speak asks for access to your device microphone. The app uses the microphone only after you begin an audio feature.

Voice recordings may be sent to our speech-processing services to provide pronunciation scoring, speech recognition, conversation responses, and related feedback. Audio is processed for the feature you requested. Do not record another person without their permission.

### Learning progress and preferences

The app may store learning progress, scores, selected topics, and interface preferences locally on your device using browser storage. This information is used to preserve your experience between sessions. It is not associated with an Emma Speak account because this version does not require account creation or sign-in.

### Technical information

Our hosting, API, and third-party service providers may receive limited technical information needed to deliver requests, such as IP address, device or browser information, request timestamps, and error information. We do not use this version of Emma Speak to sell personal information or show behavioral advertising.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

- Record and process speech that you choose to submit.
- Calculate pronunciation scores and provide feedback.
- Support AI conversation features.
- Save progress and preferences on your device.
- Maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the app and its services.

## 3. Service Providers

Emma Speak uses hosting, speech-processing, AI, and other infrastructure providers to operate its features. These providers may process information on our behalf and may have their own privacy policies and retention practices.

The app connects to a backend service for speech-to-text, pronunciation scoring, and AI conversation requests. We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services, so please review their policies where appropriate.

## 4. Information Storage and Retention

Learning progress and preferences stored locally remain on your device until you clear the app's data, remove the app, or delete the relevant browser storage.

Audio and other content sent to remote services may be temporarily processed to provide the requested result. Retention depends on the service involved and its configuration. We do not promise that a remote service will retain no data; service providers may maintain limited logs for security, reliability, billing, or abuse prevention.

## 5. Your Choices

You can:

- Deny microphone access. Pronunciation and voice conversation features will not work without it.
- Revoke microphone access later in your device settings.
- Clear local app data to remove locally stored progress and preferences.
- Stop using the app and request information about data associated with your use by contacting us.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Emma Speak is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

## 7. Security

We use HTTPS for network connections between the app and remote services. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this location with a revised effective date.
